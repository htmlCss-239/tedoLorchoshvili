About Nuts L Company

Your Trusted Partner in Premium Georgian Hazelnuts Since 2000

Who We Are

Nuts L Company was founded in 2011 and has been active in the international market of processing and export of Georgian hazelnut products. Our roots in the hazelnut processing industry go back to 2000, established by a group of people with extensive research and management experience in this business.

Located in the Samegrelo region, in the Black Sea area of western Georgia, we benefit from an ideal environment for hazelnut cultivation. Active cooperation with local farmers has enabled us to become an important player in the global market of peeled hazelnuts.

Today, Nuts L includes 2 hazelnut processing factories with a total industrial area of 30,000 m². Our facilities can process up to 5,000 tons per year, using only the best industrial equipment from Italy, Japan, and Turkey.

Quality Certifications

ISO 22000:2018 Certified

In 2018, Nuts L LLC implemented a food safety management system called Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP). We are proud holders of the ISO 22000:2018 certification, demonstrating our commitment to the highest food safety standards.

Nuts L strictly follows international standards and guidelines throughout the complete production process. Our quality management system ensures that every product meets or exceeds customer expectations and regulatory requirements.
