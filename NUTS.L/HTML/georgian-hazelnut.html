Georgian Hazelnuts

Premium Quality from the Black Sea Region

Why Georgian Hazelnuts Are Special

Georgian hazelnuts are recognized worldwide for their exceptional quality and unique flavor profile. Grown in the fertile lands of the Black Sea region, these hazelnuts benefit from ideal climate conditions, rich soil, and centuries of cultivation expertise.

The combination of perfect weather conditions, traditional farming methods, and modern processing techniques makes Georgian hazelnuts stand out in the global market. Their rich taste, high oil content, and excellent shelf life make them the preferred choice for confectionery manufacturers and food processors worldwide.

Ideal Climate & Geography

🌊

Black Sea Climate

The mild, humid climate of the Black Sea coast provides perfect conditions for hazelnut cultivation. Moderate temperatures and consistent rainfall create an ideal growing environment.

🏔️

Mountain Terrain

The hilly terrain and well-drained soils of Western Georgia offer excellent natural drainage and protection from harsh weather conditions.

🌡️

Temperature Range

Average temperatures between 12-15°C throughout the year, with cool winters and warm summers, support optimal hazelnut development.

💧

Rainfall

Annual rainfall of 1,500-2,500mm provides sufficient moisture without waterlogging, essential for healthy hazelnut orchards.

Cultivation & Harvesting

Traditional Methods

Georgian farmers have cultivated hazelnuts for centuries, passing down traditional knowledge and techniques from generation to generation. This expertise ensures the highest quality nuts.

Harvest Season

Hazelnuts are typically harvested in late August through September when the nuts have fully matured and naturally fall from the trees. This timing ensures optimal flavor and oil content.

Modern Processing

After harvest, nuts undergo modern processing including cleaning, sorting, and quality control to meet international standards while maintaining their natural qualities.

Quality Standards

Georgian hazelnuts meet the highest international quality standards and certifications.

🎯

Size & Shape

Georgian hazelnuts are known for their uniform size and round shape, making them ideal for processing and packaging.

🌟

Flavor Profile

Rich, nutty flavor with a perfect balance of sweetness and natural oils, enhancing any culinary application.

💪

High Oil Content

Georgian hazelnuts contain 60-68% oil content, contributing to their rich taste and excellent texture.

✨

Thin Shell

The relatively thin shell makes processing easier while providing adequate protection during storage and transport.

📦

Long Shelf Life

When properly stored, Georgian hazelnuts maintain their quality and flavor for extended periods.

🔬

Quality Control

Rigorous testing and sorting ensure only premium nuts reach the export market.

Health & Nutritional Benefits

Hazelnuts are not only delicious but also packed with essential nutrients that contribute to a healthy diet.

🥜 Rich in Healthy Fats

High in monounsaturated fats that support heart health and help reduce bad cholesterol levels.

💪 Protein Source

Contains approximately 15% protein, making them an excellent plant-based protein source.

🌾 High in Fiber

Rich in dietary fiber that aids digestion and promotes gut health.

💊 Vitamins & Minerals

Excellent source of Vitamin E, magnesium, copper, and B vitamins.

🛡️ Antioxidants

Contains powerful antioxidants that protect cells from damage.

❤️ Heart Health

Regular consumption supports cardiovascular health and reduces disease risk.

Culinary Uses

🍫 Confectionery

Essential ingredient in chocolate products, pralines, and candies. Georgian hazelnuts add premium taste and texture.

🍰 Baking

Perfect for cakes, cookies, pastries, and desserts. Adds rich flavor and crunchy texture to baked goods.

🥤 Beverages

Used in coffee flavoring, smoothies, and nut milk production for natural, nutty taste.

🍽️ Cooking

Adds depth to savory dishes, salads, and sauces. Roasted hazelnuts complement various cuisines.
